# Approval Policy Example

## Always Require Human Approval

- external email send
- public notice finalization
- member status downgrade
- deletion of confirmed records
- refund or payment reversal
- policy or rule interpretation with no written basis

## Allow Drafting Without Approval

- draft minutes
- draft notices
- draft emails
- dues reconciliation proposal
- membership update proposal

## Escalate to Review

- ambiguous payer match
- duplicate member candidates
- conflicting source records
- unclear owner or approver
